konomi-redteam

A red-team taxonomy that does not go stale, and a corpus that cannot become a cookbook.

Pressure is classified by the property of the system it exploits, not by the wording that exploited it — because the wording gets patched and the property does not. A collapse is recorded as a signature, never a payload. And the suite itself is gated: coverage, redundancy, missing controls, and findings that never reproduced.

Coverage becomes measurable

Five hundred rewordings of one idea look like five hundred findings and cover one cell. Identity is the named mechanism, so duplicates collapse and the empty families are reported by name.

Every pressure has a benign twin

"The model refused" is not a pass. Without a control you cannot tell a safe model from one that refuses everything, so over-refusal is a first-class finding here rather than a footnote.

The corpus is publishable by construction

There is no field for a payload, and a note carrying a transcript is refused at entry. What is kept is the structure — which is the part that stays true after the next training run.

Fill this in and the guard runs live. It is worth deliberately trying to break it — paste a transcript line, a fenced block, or a prompt into the trigger reference, and watch it be refused with the reason. That refusal is the feature.

Does this suite establish anything?

A suite passes review today by being large, and size is the one property that is trivially faked. This runs the illustrative records plus anything you added.

Each family is defined by the property it exploits. No payloads, by design — what is written down is the thing that stays true after the phrasing is patched.

Reach is one integer

Each pressure sits on one of the first seven primes, so a coverage vector [f,a,i,d,s,v,e] becomes 2^f·3^a·5^i·7^d·11^s·13^v·17^e. Because factorization is unique, a suite's reach is a single comparable number that still takes apart into exactly where it looked — two suites can be compared without averaging away the thing you wanted to know.

What the gate looks for

HoleMeaning
UNCOVEREDA pressure family has never been applied, so nothing is known about it either way.
REDUNDANTTwo records share a finding signature — one mechanism counted twice, inflating coverage.
UNCONTROLLEDA family produced breaches but no benign twin was ever run. Its results cannot be interpreted.
UNREPRODUCEDA finding rests on too few trials, or a rate that did not hold. Sampled models do things once.
OVERREFUSALA control was refused. A real failure, in the direction almost nobody reports.
STALEA record already marked fixed is still counted toward current coverage.
MONOAXIALEvery record is attributed to one axis — the suite is scoring rather than diagnosing.

Honest limits

  • Seven families is a working taxonomy, not a proof that the space has seven parts. If you find a pressure that fits none of them, the taxonomy is wrong and should change.
  • The gate checks whether a suite is interpretable. It cannot tell you whether your findings are important, and it has no opinion about any model.
  • The shipped records are illustrative and name a fictional system. None of them is an observation of any real model, and no number here should be cited.
  • Suppressing payloads is a real trade: a reader cannot reproduce your finding from the corpus alone. That is the intended cost — the trigger reference resolves inside your own store.
